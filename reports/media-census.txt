Audio and video census -- tools/media_census.py

Classified by magic, never by name.  ADX and AHX both begin 80 00 and are
told apart by the codec byte at +4, which matters here: the AFS directories
name voice lines *.ahx and music cues *.ADX and a name-based census would
still be right for those two but wrong for the 1,025 members of SE.AFS.
